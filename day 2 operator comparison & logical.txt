4.Logical Operators :

logical operators allow us to combine multiple conditions.
A logical operator is used to combine or modify Boolean expressions (True / False).

and
or
not

Operator	Meaning
and	      All conditions must be True
or	      At least one condition must be True
not	      Reverses the Boolean result


and :

T and T --> T
T and F --> F
F and T --> F
F and F --> F

or :

T or T --> T
T or F --> T
F or T --> T
F or F --> F


not :

True --> False
False --> True
